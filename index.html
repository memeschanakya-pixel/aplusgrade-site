Install in a minute

Four easy steps.

Download

Tap the yellow Download button on this page.

Open the file

Open A-Plus-Grade.apk from your notifications or Downloads folder.

Allow install

If asked, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser or Files app.

Open & start

Tap Install, open A+ Grade and enter your details to begin.

If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap “More details” → “Install anyway”. The app is shared directly by A+ Grade instead of the Play Store.
FAQ

Good to know.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. The app is for Android phones only.

I already have the old app. What now?

Just install the new file over it. If your phone says “App not installed”, uninstall the old app first and install again.

Why is screen pinning asked?

Pinning keeps you on the test screen. Tap “Got it” on the pop-up. If it doesn’t appear, turn on “Screen pinning” in your phone’s Settings.

My phone hung during a test. Did I lose it?

No. Reopen the app and your attempt resumes with the original timer.

Is it free?

Yes, it’s free for A+ Grade students.
